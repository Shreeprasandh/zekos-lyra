## 3. Unit Economics & Lifetime Value (LTV / CAC) Analysis

The unit economics of RasoiOS are exceptional due to its position as the daily mission-critical utility of the home:

### 3.1 Monthly Household Unit Economics Breakdown
* **Blended Monthly Revenue per User (ARPU)**:
  * Consumer SaaS Subscription: ₹299
  * Quick-Commerce Affiliate Commission (4.0% of ₹15k GMV): ₹600
  * FMCG Brand Sponsorship / Default Placement: ₹150
  * **Total Monthly Gross Revenue**: **₹1,049** (~$12.60)

* **Monthly Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)**:
  * Voice AI Inference (Gnani ASR/TTS, 2 sessions/day @ ₹1.50): ₹90
  * WhatsApp Business API Messaging (Meta Utility Template fees): ₹35
  * Cloud Server & Vector DB hosting (AWS / GCP / Supabase): ₹25
  * Payment Processing & Grantex Gateway fee: ₹15
  * **Total Monthly COGS**: **₹165**

* **Monthly Contribution Margin (CM)**:
  * Gross Profit: ₹1,049 - ₹165 = **₹884 per household / month**
  * **Gross Margin Percentage**: **84.3%**

### 3.2 Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) & Payback
* **Blended CAC**: ₹1,750 per household.
  * *Acquisition Channels*: Gated society activations (MyGate/NoBrokerHood targeted ads), residential apartment association partnerships, domestic cook referrals, and word-of-mouth among corporate working parents.
* **Payback Period**:
  $$\text{Payback} = \frac{\text{CAC}}{\text{Monthly CM}} = \frac{₹1,750}{₹884} \approx \mathbf{1.98 \text{ Months}}$$
* **Lifetime Value (LTV)**:
  * Assuming conservative 36-month average household lifespan (churn < 2.5% monthly due to extreme daily utility):
  $$\text{LTV} = 36 \text{ months} \times ₹884 = \mathbf{₹31,824}$$
* **LTV / CAC Ratio**:
  $$\frac{\text{LTV}}{\text{CAC}} = \frac{₹31,824}{₹1,750} = \mathbf{18.2\text{x}}$$
  *(Industry benchmark for top-tier venture capital investment is >3.0x).*

### 4.1 The Taste Memory Data Flywheel (High Switching Cost)
* A family’s culinary preferences are subtle, idiosyncratic, and non-transferable:
  * *"Rohan prefers his bhindi crispy with no onions; the grandmother eats no garlic on Thursdays; the daughter dislikes yellow dal unless tempered with hing."*
* Once RasoiOS maps these hundreds of latent parameters over 60 days, **switching to any competitor is unthinkable**, because it would mean returning to the daily morning negotiation and frustration.

### 4.2 The Domestic Cook Virality Loop
* In urban India, domestic cooks operate in localized geographic clusters (a single maid or cook typically services 4 to 6 apartments in the *exact same building or society*).
* When a cook experiences the psychological relief of RasoiOS in Apartment 402—where she never gets yelled at for making the wrong dish, where groceries are already waiting on the counter, and where instructions are spoken in her native tongue—she becomes the ultimate brand ambassador.
* She tells her employers in Apartments 201, 305, and 604: *"Bhabhi, 402 wali madam ne ek machine lagayi hai, waisa aap bhi laga lo, roz-roz poochne ka jhanjhat khatam."*
* This unlocks an unprecedented **organic, near-zero-CAC viral expansion loop**.
