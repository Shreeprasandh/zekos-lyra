## 4. Deep Architectural Breakdown of the 5 Core Engines

### 4.1 Engine 1: The Adaptive Recipe Modification & Substitution Memory
When Mom says: *"AI said put 2 cloves, but I am cooking without it because my family hates cloves"*, the system does not simply adjust today’s meal log. It activates a **Hierarchical Preference Extraction Pipeline**:

```
Spoken Utterance: "Clove mat daalo, family me kisi ko pasand nahi hai"
                                   |
                                   v
Natural Language Entity Extraction (NER & Intent Parser)
  - Target Ingredient: Syzygium aromaticum (Whole Clove / Laung)
  - Action: Negative Override (Dislike / Exclusion)
  - Scope: Global Family Level ("family me kisi ko nahi pasand")
                                   |
                                   v
Household Culinary Knowledge Graph Update
  - Node: `family.preferences.spices.clove` -> Affinity Score: 0.0 (Hard Exclusion)
  - Propagation Rule: For any recipe requiring whole warm spices (Garam Masala):
    * Excises: Whole Cloves
    * Substituted with: Green Cardamom (Elaichi) or Cinnamon (Dalchini)
    * Log Rule: "In Biryani / Pulao, omit cloves automatically without asking."
```

### 4.2 Engine 2: The Biological Decay Clock & Waste Mitigation Algorithm
Every raw ingredient entered into or deduced by Rasoi-Pod is assigned a **Biological Decay Half-Life Tensor**:

$$\tau_{\text{spoilage}} = T_{\text{purchase}} + \Delta t_{\text{category}} \times \mu_{\text{storage}}$$

* Where $\Delta t_{\text{category}}$ is the empirical shelf-life:
  * Potatoes (dry pantry): 14–21 days.
  * Tomatoes (crisper drawer): 5–7 days.
  * Coriander (refrigerator door): 2–3 days.
  * Fresh Paneer: 48–72 hours.
* **Proactive Interventions**:
  * If $\tau_{\text{remaining}} \le 48 \text{ hours}$ and remaining volume is significant (e.g., >1kg potatoes), the recommendation engine assigns a **3.5x penalty multiplier** to any recipe *not* utilizing potatoes.
  * It proactively prompts Mom: *"Mom, aapke paas 2kg aalu bache hain jo 3 din me kharab ho sakte hain. Kya aaj Aloo Gobi ya Jeera Aloo banayein?"*

### 4.3 Engine 3: The Dedicated Mobile App & Dynamic Family Synchronization
The companion mobile app is the **master identity and configuration anchor** of the household:

* **Foundational Setup & Onboarding**:
  * Homeowners configure the system once on their smartphone during BLE setup—far away from kitchen grease.
  * Captures family member profiles, medical constraints (diabetic, hypertensive), spice tolerance sliders, and Spotify credentials.
  * **The Cultural Origin vs. Location Breakthrough**: Disentangles geographic IP location from cultural culinary identity (e.g., An **Erode, Tamil Nadu** family living in **Jaipur, Rajasthan**). The engine serves Kongu Nadu / Tamil comfort foods (sambar, rasam, kootu, mor kulambu) while mapping local Jaipur market ingredient availability and substitutes.
* **Anti-Spam Dynamic Portioning**:
  * Zero daily notification spam: The system **never** sends irritating daily messages asking *"Are you coming home for dinner?"*.
  * Instead, family members can voluntarily toggle an "Eating Out" status in the app, or family members can vote on weekend specials.
  * When a member flags an absence, the wall device updates: `Portions: 3 (Rohan out)` and alerts Mom upon kitchen entry: *"Rohan bahar kha raha hai, ek portion kam banayein."*

### 4.4 Engine 4: The Palate Fatigue, Spice & Micronutrient Balancer
The system maintains a rolling 14-day nutritional and sensory profile:

1. **Spice Satiety Tracking**:
   * If dishes over the last 4 meals had a high capsaicin/chilli rating ($\text{SpiceIndex} > 7.5$), the system flags **Sensory Palate Fatigue**.
   * It recommends cooling, mild, or subtly sweet preparations: Gujarati Dal, Kadhi-Khichdi, Curd Rice, or Mild Paneer Pasanda.
2. **Macronutrient & Micronutrient Balancing**:
   * If the last 3 days were carbohydrate-heavy (Rice, Potatoes, Parathas), the recommendation engine prioritizes high-fiber and plant-protein dishes (Moong Dal Chilla, Soya Matar, Palak Dal).
   * It actively balances vitamins: if the household has consumed high Vitamin B (grains/legumes) but zero Vitamin C (citrus, capsicum, amla) for 5 days, it prioritizes recipes incorporating lemon juice, bell peppers, or amchur.

### 4.5 Engine 5: Cultural & Geographic Grounding (The Migrant Palate Graph)
The recommendation engine enforces strict **Cultural Origin over Pure Geolocation**:
* **Cultural Origin Tensor**: Defines baseline culinary grammar (e.g., Kongu Nadu Tamil: shallots, curry leaves, sesame oil, black pepper, ponni rice).
* **Local Market Substitution Adapter**: When a family resides outside their home state (e.g., Erode family in Rajasthan), the system dynamically maps local market equivalents:
  * Substitute shallots (*sambar vengayam*) with small red onions if shallots are unavailable in local Jaipur kiranas.
  * Substitute drumstick leaves (*murungai keerai*) with tender spinach/methi if unavailable.
* **Religious / Fasting Calendars**: Automatic compliance with Tamil Purattasi Saturdays (strictly vegetarian), North Indian Navratri fasting, or Jain Paryushana rules based on declared family background.

### 4.6 Engine 6: The Spotify & Ambient Kitchen Audio Pipeline
To transform the device from a sterile chore appliance into a cherished living companion:
* **Embedded Spotify Connect Engine**: Runs an embedded `librespot` audio daemon on the Rockchip SoC, allowing Mom to say: *"Rasoi, play M.S. Subbulakshmi suprabhatam"* or *"Rasoi, play 90s Bollywood cooking playlist"*.
* **Hardware-Loopback Acoustic Echo Cancellation (AEC)**:
  * When the 5W Neodymium speaker is playing music at 75dB, standard microphones become deafened.
  * Rasoi-Pod incorporates a dedicated audio DSP with hardware-reference loopback that subtracts the amplifier line-out signal from the microphone input in real time.
  * Mom can easily whisper *"Rasoi, volume down"* or *"Next"* even while music is blaring.
* **Tactile Rotary Wheel Integration**:
  * Mom can nudge the knurled rotary wheel with an elbow or single greasy finger to adjust volume or tap to pause without smudging the glass display.
* **Audio Tiers & Free Radio Fallback**:
  * For households without Spotify Premium, the system natively integrates free internet radio streams (TuneIn, Prasar Bharati, Devotional Chants) and Bluetooth Audio streaming.
